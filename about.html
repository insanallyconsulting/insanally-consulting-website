Helping businesses get found, and get chosen.

Insanally Consulting is led by Marc Insanally. Through SEO and branding, it helps businesses show up when customers search, and look like the right choice when they do. The practice is rooted in the Greater Toronto Area, and the same approach works wherever your business and customers are.

Why SEO and branding go together

Showing up in search gets a customer to look at you. What they see next (your website, your Google profile, your business card, the sign out front) decides whether they call. Doing both means the work that gets you found isn't lost on a first impression that doesn't hold up.

The focus is on businesses where that moment turns directly into a phone call, a booking, or someone walking through the door. That direct connection to revenue is what makes the work worth doing properly and worth doing honestly.

How this work gets done

Research before recommendations. SEO targets come from actual search data and competitor research, and design choices come from your business and your customers. Nothing is decided in advance.

Plain language. You'll hear about outcomes for your business, not technical jargon you have to translate yourself.

Honest about limits. No guaranteed rankings, no inflated promises, and no results shown that haven't actually been earned by a client.

Structured, not improvised. Every project follows the same clear process (discovery call, review, proposal, delivery) so nothing gets lost along the way.

Consistent from start to finish. Your search presence and your brand are built to match, so customers see the same business everywhere they find you.

Curious where your business stands?

A short discovery call is the best way to find out: no obligation, just a clear look at where the opportunities are, in search and in how your business looks.
