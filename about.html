SEO for businesses in the GTA and beyond.

Insanally Consulting is led by Marc Insanally, helping businesses get found by the customers already searching for them. The practice is rooted in the Greater Toronto Area, but the same research-driven approach applies wherever your business and customers are located.

Why local businesses

These are businesses where a good search result turns directly into a phone call, a booking, or someone walking through the door. That direct connection between visibility and revenue is what makes SEO worth doing properly and worth doing honestly.

How this work gets done

Research before recommendations. Keyword targets and page structures come from actual search data and competitor research, never decided in advance.

Plain language. You'll hear about outcomes for your business, not technical jargon you have to translate yourself.

Honest about limits. No guaranteed rankings, no inflated promises, and no results shown that haven't actually been earned by a client.

Structured, not improvised. Every engagement follows the same clear process (audit, roadmap, implementation, reporting) so nothing gets lost along the way.
