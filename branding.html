Why branding matters for a small business

When your colours, fonts and logo change from one place to the next, customers notice, even if they can't say why. It can make a good business look less established than it really is.

A brand kit fixes that. It sets out one clear set of rules for how your business looks, so your website, Google profile, business cards, banners and social media all feel like the same business. It also saves you time, because every printer or designer you work with gets the same guide.

Common questions

Do you design logos?

Your existing logo can be refined and set up properly as part of a brand kit. Designing a logo from scratch is quoted separately.

I'm a small business. Do I really need a brand kit?

If you have a website, a Google listing, business cards or a sign, you already have a brand. A brand kit just makes sure they all match, which helps customers recognize and trust you.

How many revisions are included?

Each design service includes 2 rounds of revisions.

Can you also design my business cards and signs?

Yes. Business cards, banners and signage, flyers and menus are all available, and they can be designed to match your brand kit. See print design.
