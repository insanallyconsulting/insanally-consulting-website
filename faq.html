Common questions, answered plainly.

A few things people usually want to know before booking a discovery call. If your question isn't here, just ask.

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Local SEO is a gradual process. Most businesses start seeing meaningful movement over a few months of consistent work, not days. That's part of why the Monthly Growth option exists: sustained effort is what actually moves rankings.

Do you also handle Google Ads or paid search?

Not currently. This practice focuses specifically on SEO. Paid search and organic search do different jobs, and the two can actually complement each other well if you're already running ads elsewhere.

Do you work with businesses outside the Greater Toronto Area?

The practice is based in the GTA, but the work isn't limited to it. If your business is elsewhere, reach out: the same research-driven approach applies wherever your customers are searching.

What happens on the discovery call?

We talk through your business, your customers, and what "more business" would actually look like for you. No preparation needed on your end.

Can you guarantee my business will rank #1?

No one can honestly guarantee specific rankings. What you get instead is a strategy built on real data and consistent execution, with clear reporting on what's changing and why.

What's the difference between the Audit and Setup & Implementation options?

The Audit gives you a full review and prioritized recommendations you can act on yourself or hand off to someone else. Setup & Implementation includes everything in the Audit, plus the fixes and optimizations actually put in place directly.
