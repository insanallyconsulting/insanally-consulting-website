Common questions, answered plainly.

A few things people usually want to know before booking a discovery call. If your question isn't here, just ask.

Working together

What services do you offer?

Two kinds of work that go together. SEO helps customers find you: audits, website and Google Business Profile fixes, monthly SEO, and replies to your Google reviews. Branding and print help them choose you: brand kits, business cards, banners and signage, and flyers and menus. See all services.

Can I get SEO and branding together?

Yes. Many businesses need both, and doing them together means your search presence and your brand match everywhere customers find you. You can also start with one and add the other later.

What happens on the discovery call?

We talk through your business, your customers, and what "more business" would actually look like for you. No preparation needed on your end.

Do you work with businesses outside the Greater Toronto Area?

The practice is based in the GTA, but the work isn't limited to it. If your business is elsewhere, reach out: the same approach applies wherever your customers are.

How does payment work?

Payment is by Interac e-Transfer. Pricing and payment terms are set out in your proposal and agreement before any work begins. See the refund policy for details.

SEO and Google reviews

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

Local SEO is a gradual process. Most businesses start seeing meaningful movement over a few months of consistent work, not days. That's part of why the Monthly SEO option exists: sustained effort is what actually moves rankings.

Can you guarantee my business will rank #1?

No one can honestly guarantee specific rankings. What you get instead is a strategy built on real data and consistent execution, with clear reporting on what's changing and why.

What's the difference between the Audit and Setup & Implementation options?

The Audit gives you a full review and prioritized recommendations you can act on yourself or hand off to someone else. Setup & Implementation includes everything in the Audit, plus the fixes and optimizations actually put in place directly. More on the SEO page.

Can you reply to my Google reviews for me?

Yes. Review Management covers replies to new Google reviews, written in your voice and checked several times a week. You approve any reply to a negative review before it is posted. It is available on its own or with any Monthly SEO level.

Do you also handle Google Ads or paid search?

Not currently. This practice focuses on SEO and branding. Paid search and organic search do different jobs, and the two can actually complement each other well if you're already running ads elsewhere.

Branding and print

Do you design logos?

Your existing logo can be refined and set up properly as part of a brand kit. Designing a logo from scratch is quoted separately.

How many revisions are included?

Each design service includes 2 rounds of revisions.

Do you handle the printing?

I design the files and set them up so they are ready to print. You can send them to any print shop or online printer, and I can recommend one if you need it. See print design.

What files will I get?

Print-ready PDFs with the correct size, bleed and colour settings, plus image versions for sharing online and on social media.
