Common questions

Do you handle the printing?

I design the files and set them up so they are ready to print. You can send them to any print shop or online printer, and I can recommend one if you need it.

What files will I get?

Print-ready PDFs with the correct size, bleed and colour settings, plus image versions for sharing online.

How many revisions are included?

Each design service includes 2 rounds of revisions.

I don't have a brand yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Print pieces can be designed from your existing logo and colours, or alongside a brand kit so everything matches from the start.

Need something designed for print?

Tell me what you need on a short discovery call, and you'll get a clear proposal for the work.
