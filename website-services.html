Why your website needs attention

Before most customers call, they look you up. What they find on your website (how it looks, whether the information is right, how easy it is to use on a phone) shapes whether they reach out or move on.

Websites also drift out of date. Hours change, services change, staff and prices change, and small things break over time. Website Services cover both: a revamp when your site needs a fresh start, and regular check-ups so it stays accurate.

Common questions

What's the difference between a Website Revamp and Website Maintenance?

A Website Revamp is a one-time project that gives your site a complete new look, a cleaner layout and refreshed content. Website Maintenance is a check-up every 6 months that keeps your site accurate, tidy and working. Many businesses start with a revamp and then keep the site current with maintenance, but each service works on its own.

Can you work on my current website?

Most likely. I work with most common website platforms, and we'll confirm yours on the discovery call. I'll just need access to your site to make the changes.

Will a revamp help my site show up on Google?

A revamp includes basic on-page SEO cleanup, so the new site is set up to be found. Ongoing work to improve your rankings is a separate service. See SEO.

How many revisions are included?

Each revamp includes 2 rounds of revisions.

Is your website due for a refresh?
