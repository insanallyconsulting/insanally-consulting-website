Privacy Policy

How this website and this business collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: September 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to insanallyconsulting.com and to Insanally Consulting, a sole proprietorship operated by Marc Insanally, based in Ontario, Canada. It explains what information is collected through this website and through the course of client work, and how that information is used.

Information collected through this website

When you submit the contact form on this site, the following information is collected: your name, business name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the message you enter. This information is sent by Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, to Insanally Consulting's business email. It is used solely to respond to your inquiry and, if you proceed, to begin the discovery process outlined on the Services page.

This site also uses Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Search Console to understand general visitor traffic and search performance, such as which pages are viewed and which search terms bring visitors to the site. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors personally.

This site does not use advertising cookies or sell visitor data to third parties.

Information collected as a client

If you become a client, additional information may be collected through the Client Intake Questionnaire and related documents, such as business details, website and analytics access, and marketing goals. This information is used only to plan, deliver, and report on the SEO services outlined in your service agreement, and is not shared outside of that engagement without your consent, except as required to deliver the service itself (for example, granting Google the access you've authorized for Search Console or Analytics).

Third-party services

This site and this business rely on a small number of third-party services to operate, including Web3Forms (contact form delivery), Google Analytics and Google Search Console (site and search performance), and Cloudflare (hosting and domain infrastructure). Each of these providers processes limited data as described above and maintains its own privacy practices.

How information is stored

Client information is stored securely and is only accessible to Marc Insanally. Information is retained for as long as needed to deliver services and to meet reasonable business and tax record-keeping obligations, and is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

Your choices

You can ask, at any time, what information is held about you, request a correction, or request that your information be deleted, subject to any records that must be kept for legal, tax, or contractual reasons. You can also opt out of further contact at any point.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in how this business or website operates. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to insanally.consulting@gmail.com.
